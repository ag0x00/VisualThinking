Any psychology / perception / aesthetics claim sourced from research literature should explicitly state **what populations the claim was validated on**. WEIRD (Western, Educated, Industrialized, Rich, Democratic) sample-only validation is the dominant pattern in psychology research; treat it as a default flag, not as evidence of universality.

**Why:** User-aligned Phase 3 audit (2026-05-17) found that **cross-cultural variation is the most-reliable falsifier of canonical claims** in perception-psychology. Every Phase 3 anchor that had cross-cultural data showed substantial variation:

- **Russell's affect circumplex** — the valence-arousal V-shape is steeper in Western (Canadian, Spanish) than East Asian (Korean, Japanese) samples; the Hong Kong sample showed valence-arousal *independence*, not orthogonality-on-a-circle. The "circumplex" geometry is a Western finding, not a human universal.
- **Face universality** — Crivelli & Gendron's Himba (2014) and Trobriand (2017) studies showed free-labeling agreement with Ekman emotion-categories at chance levels for several emotions.
- **Color symbolism** — extensively documented variation in Phase 1; "red = passion" is Western; in China red is celebration/wedding; in some African contexts it's mourning.
- **Perceptual constants** — Müller-Lyer illusion magnitude varies cross-culturally (Segall 1966).
- **Emotion categories** — Barrett's [[Constructed Emotion Theory|constructionist account]] grounds variation in cultural emotion-concepts.

The pattern that holds across all of these: **universal substrate + cultural overlay**. Some basic substrate (V/A core affect; valence in face response; basic color perception) appears universal; the specific categorical / symbolic / geometric overlay is cultural.

**How to apply:**

- When citing an empirical psychology / perception / aesthetics claim, ask: "What population was this validated on?" If WEIRD-only, mark it in the wiki text. Don't pretend universality.
- Default to **dimensional / structural specifications** over **categorical universal claims**. Dimensional ratings travel better than category labels.
- For **branding work targeted at specific cultural markets**, do not assume Western framings hold. Audit categorical assumptions in target markets.
- For **generative art with figural / emotional content**, prefer (V, A) coordinates or FACS-anchored specifications over basic-emotion category labels.
- For **color work**, prefer perceptual coordinates ([[The Munsell and CIELAB Color Systems|OKLCH / CIELAB]]) over named-color-with-symbolism.
