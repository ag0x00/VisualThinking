**The 15-gap depth-dive priority queue** (user-set 2026-05-17):

1. **Emotion psychology** — Plutchik's wheel, Russell's affect circumplex (valence/arousal), James-Lange, Ekman's basic emotions, FACS.
2. **Color psychology** — color-emotion associations, cultural variation, Goethe's *Theory of Colours*. Distinct from the wiki's existing colorimetry pages.
3. **Empirical aesthetics** as a field — Fechner (founder, 1876 *Vorschule der Aesthetik*), Berlyne (1971 *Aesthetics and Psychobiology*, arousal-potential theory), Palmer & Schloss (Berkeley), Vessel (neuroaesthetics).
4. **Algorithmic composition** as a field — Nake, Mohr, Cohen's AARON, Galanter's *Generative Art Theory*. Directly serves project 1.
5. **Perceptual constants** — size, shape, color constancy; visual illusions (Müller-Lyer, Ebbinghaus) as windows into perception.
6. **Visual hierarchy / typography** — scale, weight, contrast, position; type as voice. Foundational for projects 2 and 3.
7. **Negative space** as first-class compositional tool.
8. **Time-based composition** — Eisenstein's montage theory, McCloud's comics theory, animation pacing, frame rhythm. Directly serves project 4 and dynamic art in project 1.
9. **Movement, rhythm, repetition with variation** — visual flow, anchoring vs. variation.
10. **Light vocabulary beyond chiaroscuro** — key/fill/back, rim, three-point, motivated practical light, atmospheric perspective.
11. **Body language / pose semantics** — confident vs. defeated vs. threatening; contrapposto; status cues.
12. **Face perception** — face-specialness in vision, FACS (overlap with #1), micro-expressions, uncanny valley.
13. **Materials and texture** — perceived weight/warmth/"thingness"; wood vs. glass vs. metal as triggers.
14. **Style as system** — Impressionism, Cubism, Bauhaus as rule-sets; style transfer.
15. **Cultural / symbolic / iconographic** — religious, archetypal (Jungian), brand-semiotic.
