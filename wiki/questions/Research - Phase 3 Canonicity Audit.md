# Research — Phase 3 Canonicity Audit (Option C)

## Overview

Phase 3 of the Discovery Methodology Fix (`wiki/meta/Discovery Methodology Plan.md`). Conservative scope per locked decision: audit only the **load-bearing canonical claims** in the Affect Foundations and L1 Cleanup sweeps for the same overclaim pattern that Phase 1 caught in the Algorithmic Composition + Tools sweep. Goal: confirm or revise; don't re-do.

## Methodology

8 parallel WebSearches: 2 per audit target, covering known critique literature for each canonical claim. No new tool sweeps; this is targeted contestation discovery + page revision.

## The four targets and findings

### 1. [[Berlyne's Arousal-Potential Theory]] — major revision

**Phase 1 surfaced** the "mostly abandoned" claim from IEP *Empirical Aesthetics*. Phase 3 confirms and extends:

- **Specific reasons for abandonment**: arousal cannot account for emotion diversity in art perception; the arousal construct is neurophysiologically under-supported; experiments failed to confirm predictions; semantic factors (meaning, association) — not collative variables (complexity, novelty) — drove preferences.
- **Successor theories**: [[Processing Fluency Theory|processing fluency]] (Reber et al. 2004), contemporary neuroaesthetics (Vessel, Leder & Nadal), and predictive-processing (Van de Cruys 2017). The wiki's earlier framing positioned predictive-processing as Berlyne's continuation; it's better read as a *replacement* with a different mechanism.
- **Empirical record on inverted-U specifically is mixed**: replicates for skeletal-complexity of shapes (Sun & Firestone 2022); "scant evidence" in product design (Marin et al. 2021); the pattern is *not* robust across stimulus types.

**Revision applied**: top-of-page audit callout + new "Critique: the framework has been substantially abandoned since the 2000s" section + downgraded "central theoretical pillar" language throughout.

### 2. [[Russell's Affect Circumplex]] — major revision

- **Geometry is not cross-culturally universal**. Kuppens et al. and successors find the V-shape between valence and arousal is steeper in Western (Canadian, Spanish) than East Asian (Korean, Japanese) samples; the Hong Kong sample fits a straight line (independence, not orthogonal-on-a-circle).
- **Personality affects geometry** (extraverts steeper; introverts weaker).
- **Constructionist alternative** ([[Constructed Emotion Theory|Barrett 2017]]) reframes the circumplex as a *representational* structure (how emotion words are sorted) rather than as the *causal* structure of emotion.
- **Ellipse-rather-than-circumplex** finding (Klimek-Trochim et al. 2021) — the two dimensions are not equally weighted; valence carries more variance.
- **Discrete-emotion alternatives** ([[Plutchik's Wheel of Emotions|Plutchik]], Ekman) and **3D models** ([[PAD Emotion Model|PAD]]) remain serious competitors.

**Revision applied**: top-of-page audit callout + new "Critique: cross-cultural and empirical limits" section + downgraded "most-empirically-validated" framing.

### 3. [[Helmholtz Gibson and Bayesian Perception]] — moderate revision

- **Synthesis is widely-adopted but contested.** Pure ecological-psychology (Turvey, Chemero) rejects the inferentialist framing entirely. "The myth of the Bayesian brain" (2024-26 review) collects empirical and conceptual challenges.
- **Friston's free-energy / predictive-processing** has been criticized as "so general as to be unfalsifiable" (Williams 2022, Synthese).
- **Compatibility-not-victory reading**: Orlandi (2015) argues Bayesian perception is *compatible with* ecological perception under a non-constructivist reading — i.e., neither side wins; both describe the same phenomenon.
- **Engineering practice survives** (ML uses both paradigms productively); **theoretical consensus** does not.

**Revision applied**: top-of-page note + new "Critique: the synthesis is defensible, not settled" section + softened "modern resolution" language.

### 4. [[Face Recognition Universality Debate]] — minor revision (already well-balanced)

The page already presents both Ekman and Barrett camps fairly. Phase 3 adds the **third framing**:

- **Behavioral Ecology View (BECV)** — Crivelli & Fridlund 2018. Facial displays are *functional signals* in social interaction, not readouts of internal emotion states (contra Ekman) and not primarily constructed-categorizations (contra Barrett's strong framing). The debate is three-way, not two-way.
- Keltner et al. (2019) counter-pushback against BECV exists.

**Revision applied**: new "The behavioral-ecology alternative (third framing)" section.

## Key findings across the four targets

1. **The "central theoretical pillar" framing is now fully retired.** The wiki's earlier claim that Galanter's effective-complexity = Berlyne's arousal-potential = Russell's circumplex = a unified canonical foundation is no longer defensible at any of the three layers. All three pages now carry critique sections.
2. **The "universal substrate + cultural overlay" pattern survives.** The Phase 1 finding (cross-cultural color, perceptual constants, face universality, emotion categorization) is reinforced by Phase 3's cross-cultural circumplex finding. The wiki's *operational* recommendation — *prefer dimensional specification with explicit cultural caveats over categorical universal claims* — is reinforced by every framing examined.
3. **Berlyne is the largest single downgrade.** Of the four anchors, only Berlyne has been *substantially abandoned* by mainstream literature. The other three are *contested* but still actively defended. Berlyne is now positioned as historical infrastructure, not a live framework.
4. **Predictive-processing has its own unfalsifiability concerns.** The wiki's prior implicit reliance on predictive-processing as a *settled* successor to Berlyne is itself questionable. The successor framings are processing fluency (better-evidenced) + neuroaesthetics (broader but more empirical-than-theoretical) + predictive-processing (programmatic, partly unfalsifiable).
5. **The Behavioral Ecology View deserves naming.** Ekman-vs-Barrett dominates discourse but a *functional-signaling* alternative is a third defensible framing for face-emotion work. Re-frames the design question from "what emotion does this face express?" to "what social function does this face serve?"

## Methodology notes for Phase 4 lock-in

- **Pattern recognized**: every framework presented as "*the* canonical theory of X" deserved re-examination. Three of four had legitimate contestation; the fourth was already balanced. The base rate of canonicity-overclaim across the wiki was meaningful (75% of the audited anchors needed revision).
- **The "central theoretical pillar" framing is dangerous in general**. Whenever the wiki uses unification language ("X = Y = the spine of the framework"), it should be flagged for audit.
- **Cross-cultural variation is the most-reliable falsifier of canonical claims** in psychology-of-perception domains. Any framework that has only been empirically validated on WEIRD samples should carry an explicit cultural-validity caveat.
- **Successor theories matter**: the wiki should track not just whether a framework was once dominant but *what replaced it* and why. Berlyne → processing fluency is the cleanest example; the wiki now has both.

These notes feed directly into Phase 4 methodology lock-in.
