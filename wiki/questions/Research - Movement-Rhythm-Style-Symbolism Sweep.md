## Key findings

1. **The Hat monotile (March 2023) is the named successor to Penrose tilings.** Smith, Myers, Kaplan, Goodman-Strauss discovered a *single shape* (the Hat) that aperiodically tiles the plane. The Spectres family (May 2023) extended to chiral monotiles (no mirror needed). 50-year einstein problem closed. Most significant tessellation-mathematics event since Penrose 1974.

2. **Jung's archetype theory is not accepted in mainstream psychology.** Lacks empirical support; "more psychological mythos than testable hypothesis"; not part of standard curricula since ~1970s. **Mark-Pearson 12 brand archetypes inherit this** — widely used in industry, but the empirical validation is post-hoc qualitative, not falsifiable. The wiki treats this carefully: useful as *team vocabulary*, not as psychological science.

3. **Eisenstein, Wölfflin, and Panofsky all have substantial named successor critiques.** Bordwell's neo-formalism (Eisenstein), new art history / Bryson + Bal (Wölfflin), visual culture studies / Belting + Mitchell (Panofsky). The pattern from Phase 3 audit (75% base rate of canonicity overclaim in pre-2000 anchors) replicates here.

4. **IP-Adapter + ControlNet (2023) + ICAS (2025) substantially supersede Gatys (2015) for style transfer.** Diffusion-era stack is categorically different — cross-attention conditioning vs. Gram-matrix gradient optimization. Production work uses the diffusion-era stack; Gatys remains pedagogically useful.

5. **Bonner's polygonal technique** is the canonical historical construction method for Islamic geometric patterns (Bonner 2017). Most diverse and complex IGPs use this *behind-the-scenes polygonal tessellation* approach. Lu-Steinhardt 2007's "girih tiles produce quasiperiodic patterns" claim is contested but widely-cited.

6. **Disney 12 animation principles are the most-validated framework in this sweep.** 40+ years across medium shifts (cel → 3D → CGI → motion graphics → UI motion) without fundamental revision. Thesen 2020 reviewed and updated; no major changes needed.

7. **Design systems are the named successor to PDF brand guidelines.** Material Design, Apple HIG, IBM Carbon, Shopify Polaris are *operational* (code + tokens + components), not just *documentary* (PDF + spec). The transition happened ~2010-2020.

8. **Non-Western iconography is under-served in default LLM behavior.** Multimodal LLMs default to Western-canon iconographic readings unless explicitly redirected. Western canon (Christian + Greco-Roman + heraldic) is well-handled; Buddhist / Hindu / Chinese / Japanese are weaker; indigenous systems substantially weaker. Convention #5 (cross-cultural validity) requires explicit redirection for non-Western work.

9. **Aspect-to-aspect transitions in manga** vs Western comics is one of the more-replicated cross-cultural comics findings. Manga uses aspect-to-aspect (one moment, multiple views) far more — possibly linked to *ma* aesthetic ([[Ma and Yohaku no Bi]]). Wiki convention #5 confirmed.

10. **The 70ms causality threshold** ([[Phenomenal Causality]]) is consistent with the cross-modal-binding window in the audio-visual perception literature. Independent confirmation of a finding from Arnheim Sweep 3.
