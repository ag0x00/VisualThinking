## Key findings

1. **Variable fonts at 98% browser support 2026** (per CanIUse). Bringhurst-era type-pairing rules are *partially obsolete* — a single variable family with weight/width/slant/optical-size/grade axes replaces what used to require 6-10 separate typefaces. **The single largest typography shift since print→web.**

2. **New Naturalism (Lubezki/Deakins/Chivo) is the named successor to three-point lighting.** Three-point remains pedagogically canonical but contemporary practice favors single motivated source + practicals heavy + darkness as narrative material. *The Revenant* (2015) was shot entirely natural light. Generative-art systems should expose both modes.

3. **ACES 2.0 (2024) supersedes ACES 1.0** for HDR/SDR cinematic tone-mapping. Less aggressive tone scale, better hue preservation, multi-display consistency. DaVinci Resolve 20 has enhanced ACES 2.0 support; three.js / Blender adoption is progressing. For new generative-art work, use ACES 2.0 if your renderer supports it.

4. **Gatys 2015 neural texture synthesis** has partly displaced Heeger-Simoncelli statistical features for *operational* material recognition and texture synthesis. Procedural (Perlin, Worley, reaction-diffusion) and neural lineages **coexist** in 2026; procedural is fast/parametric/deterministic, neural is high-fidelity/photoreal/heavy. Production work uses both.

5. **Tufte's data-ink ratio is empirically contested.** Most studies show user preference for non-minimalist bar-graphs ("chartjunk"); high data-ink ratio can hurt readability; Tufte overlooks memorability and engagement. Pattern parallel to [[Berlyne's Arousal-Potential Theory|Berlyne]] (1980s canonical 1-author principle with mixed empirical support). Treat as practitioner heuristic, not settled empirical principle.

6. **Multilingual typography is the wiki's single most-under-acknowledged cultural-anchoring case.** Swiss grid, Bringhurst principles, type voice, F-pattern reading are *all* Latin-script-and-LTR-anchored. Arabic / Hebrew RTL reverses the F-pattern entirely (top-right primary, not top-left). CJK has its own grid traditions (genkō yōshi) and weight-matching considerations. Convention #5 (cross-cultural validity) treats this as a default flag.

7. **Material perception is well-served by the 2014 Fleming framework** at the perceptual level; the *operational* state-of-art has shifted to neural-network material recognition (Bell-Liu-Bala MINC 2015, MatPredict 2025). Both lineages remain useful.

8. **Ma (間) ≠ "negative space"** in the simple Western sense. Ma is temporal + spatial + relational; *yohaku no bi* (余白の美) is the more-2D-visual cognate. Isozaki's 1978 Paris exhibition is the canonical primary anchor. Western "minimalism" often flattens these into a pure visual-aesthetic principle, stripping the meditative/temporal dimension.

9. **Skeuomorphism → flat-design → glassmorphism → post-flat** trajectory in screen materiality is well-documented in practitioner discourse. The contemporary equilibrium (2024+) is **selective materiality** — most UI flat, specific moments (modals, hero cards) carry materiality cues. Glassmorphism via CSS `backdrop-filter` is the dominant materiality cue in 2026.

10. **Font tooling on npm is thin** (mirrors [[PCG Toolkit|PCG]] and [[Postdigital Tools|postdigital]] pattern: service-driven not package-driven). Google Fonts / Adobe Fonts / Cloudflare Fonts / Fontshare dominate; fontfaceobserver and a few utilities are the only mature npm options. Discovery for typography tools should go beyond npm.
