# Build Working Loop

The project's standard six-step loop for any substantive **build** task (a generator, a demo, a
screensaver pass, a toolkit operator). It is the sibling of [[Wiki Methodology]] (which governs
*reading sweeps*); this governs *making things*. Adopted 2026-06-21 after an audit found a demo
whose aesthetics were all decided ad-hoc with the wiki never consulted — violating the project's
own premise that **the wiki is a participant** and **the workflow is the product**.

## The loop

1. **Understand** — restate the task; place it in the [[Application Priorities|four priorities]].
2. **Consult the wiki FIRST** *(mandatory before any design)* — `wiki_orient` + `wiki_search`
   (pair them; `orient` under-surfaces tradition-specific terms), read the relevant
   concept/technique pages, and extract the applicable **operators/principles**. If you are making
   aesthetic decisions (palette, composition, gesture, pacing) and have not opened the wiki, stop.
3. **Research the gaps** the wiki does not cover — targeted web research, then **file new wiki
   pages** so the vault grows. Research feeds the wiki, not just the task.
4. **Work through aesthetics explicitly** — translate principles into concrete **targets/knobs
   before coding**: palette (colour tools), composition ([[Ma and Yohaku no Bi|Ma]] /
   [[Negative Space Techniques|negative-space]]), gesture ([[Organic vs Mechanical Motion]] /
   [[Directed Tension]]), and the **evaluation metrics** you will grade against. This is the step
   most often skipped.
5. **Build** the minimal implementation against those targets (ponytail — laziest thing that hits
   the targets).
6. **Evaluate** against the metrics (generate → score → improve), then **feed learnings + new pages
   back to the wiki** and commit.

## Why these steps

The loop formalizes existing project memories into one procedure:
[[feedback_new-project-learning-workflow|new-project learning workflow]] (read wiki → targeted
autoresearch → adapt), [[feedback_test-artifact-vs-workflow|test-artifact ≠ goal]] (the workflow is
the deliverable), the operational [[Operational Readiness Registry|Perceive/Build/Evaluate]]
standard, and [[feedback_design-for-consumer|design for the consumer]]. Plan mode is a natural host
for steps 1–4; exiting plan mode is the gate into 5–6.

## Worked example

The first run of this loop: [[Research - Ink and Watercolor Simulation on Paper|ink simulation]] →
the `screensaver/ink-metal/` Metal build → natural Chinese-ink strokes, where step 2 surfaced
[[Ma and Yohaku no Bi]], [[Organic vs Mechanical Motion]], [[Directed Tension]] and step 3 filed
[[Chinese Brushwork Principles]].
